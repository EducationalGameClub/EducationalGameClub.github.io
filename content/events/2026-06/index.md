### About Universe & More
In [their own words](https://universeandmore.com/about):

> Universe and More is a nonprofit organization dedicated to making physics education free, accessible, and genuinely engaging for students everywhere.
> 
> Our interactive physics games and simulations help students build intuition for core concepts through exploration and play. By testing ideas and seeing results directly, students develop understanding in a way that feels natural.
> 
> Our goal is simple: use what games do best&mdash;foster curiosity, encourage experimentation, and provide immediate feedback&mdash;to support real learning.

They've made free games on a range of physics topics including magnetism, energy, waves, kinematics, electric circuits, and electrostatics.

Check out [their full collection of 8 "games that make physics click"](https://universeandmore.com/)!
